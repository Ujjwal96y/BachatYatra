For Beginners

How to Start Investing
as a Student

01

Open a Demat Account

Register with CDSC Nepal to get your Demat account. It's free for students and takes under 15 minutes online.

02

Start with Mutual Funds

Low risk, professionally managed. Start a SIP with as little as ₨500/month through funds like Nabil Balanced Fund.

03

Explore NEPSE Stocks

Once comfortable, buy shares of strong sectors — hydropower, banking, insurance. Research before buying.

04

Track & Rebalance

Use BachatYatra's tracker to monitor growth and rebalance your portfolio every 6 months for best returns.
