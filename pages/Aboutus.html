Welcome to Expense Tracker, a user-friendly financial management platform designed to help individuals manage their money more effectively. Our website provides tools and resources for tracking expenses, planning savings, exploring investment options, and staying updated with financial information. Our goal is to promote financial awareness and help users make informed decisions about their income, expenses, and future financial plans. By offering features such as expense tracking, savings guidance, investment information, and financial news, we aim to make personal finance management simple and accessible for everyone. This project was developed by a team of sttudents as part of an academic project, combining technology and financial management to create a practical and informative platform. We are committed to helping users build better financial habits and achieve their financial goals. Thank you for choosing Expense Tracker as your partner in financial planning and management.
